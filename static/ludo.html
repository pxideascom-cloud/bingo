    THE HOUSE RULES

    Four tokens.
    One race home.

    1. Choose your table.

      Create a room for 2 or 4 players and invite your friends. Two-player games use red and yellow. Everyone must be online and ready to start.

    2. Roll and move.

      Roll a six to bring a token out of its yard. Click a highlighted token on the board or use the token buttons. Each roll moves one token.

    3. Catch your friends.

      Land on an opponent outside a starred safe square to send their token back to its yard. Tokens can share squares; there are no blockades in this version.

    4. Get home.

      After a circuit, your token follows its color's home lane. You need an exact roll to finish. The first player with all four tokens home wins.

    A six, a capture, or reaching home earns another roll. Three consecutive sixes end your turn; the third six is discarded. If you have no legal move, the turn passes automatically (a six still earns another roll). A disconnected player pauses the game for up to five minutes. Everyone votes for a rematch.
