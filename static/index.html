THE HOUSE RULES

Five lines.
One glorious Bingo.

  1. Bring a friend.

    Create a private room and share its link or six-character code. Each player gets a different shuffled 5 × 5 card with numbers 1–25.

  2. Make it official.

    Shuffle your card as much as you like, then press “I'm ready.” The game starts when both players are ready.

  3. Take your turn.

    Click any uncalled number on your card. It gets marked on both cards automatically. The room creator calls first; you alternate turns.

  4. Collect five lines.

    A full row, column, or diagonal earns a letter in B–I–N–G–O. Reach five lines to win. If both players reach five on the same call, it's a draw!

Lost connection? Reopen the same tab within five minutes to keep your seat. For a rematch, both players choose “Play again”; the starting player alternates.
